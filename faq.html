FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything we're asked most often about pricing, deposits, pain, healing, and getting ready for your appointment.

Price & Deposits

Can I ask for a price estimate?

You can definitely ask for a price estimate on your tattoo. However, it may be more effective to give your budget to the artist so they can work within your means and provide options that fit your financial range.

Are tattoo prices negotiable?

Tattoo prices can be negotiable in certain cases, especially for custom tattoos. However, fixed prices are common for flash tattoos or when an artist has a set hourly rate. It's important to respect the artist's valuation of their work while discussing pricing, and negotiations may impact the complexity or size of the tattoo.

What are tattoo deposits for?

Tattoo deposits serve multiple purposes. They secure your appointment slot in the artist's calendar, demonstrate your commitment to the appointment, and protect the artist in case of no-shows or last-minute cancellations. Deposits are typically deducted from the final tattoo cost.

Other Questions

Is getting a tattoo painful?

Pain levels vary depending on the individual's pain tolerance and the placement of the tattoo. Generally, areas with more nerve endings or thinner skin may be more sensitive.

How long does a tattoo take to heal?

The healing process typically takes 2-3 weeks. During this time, it's essential to follow aftercare instructions provided by your tattoo artist to ensure proper healing and minimize complications.

Can I bring a design for my tattoo?

Yes, you can bring your own design or references to your tattoo appointment. Your tattoo artist can work with you to modify the design or create a custom tattoo based on your ideas.

How do I choose the right tattoo artist?

Research different tattoo artists and studios to find one that specializes in the style you want. Look at portfolios, read reviews, and consider scheduling a consultation to discuss your ideas with the artist.

Is it safe to get a tattoo?

When done by a professional and licensed tattoo artist using sterile equipment, getting a tattoo is generally safe. It's crucial to choose a reputable studio and follow proper aftercare guidelines to minimize risks of infection or complications.

Can I get a tattoo if I have allergies or skin conditions?

It's important to inform your tattoo artist about any allergies or skin conditions you may have before getting a tattoo. They can advise you on whether it's safe to proceed and may recommend a patch test in certain cases.

How much does a tattoo cost?

Tattoo prices vary depending on factors such as the size, complexity, and artist's experience. It's best to discuss pricing with your tattoo artist during a consultation, where they can provide an estimate based on your design and requirements.

Can I remove or cover up an existing tattoo?

Yes, tattoo removal or cover-up is possible, but it may require multiple sessions and specialized techniques. Consult with a tattoo removal specialist or cover-up artist for personalized advice and options.

How should I prepare for my tattoo appointment?

Follow any pre-tattoo instructions provided by your artist, such as avoiding alcohol or certain medications. Stay hydrated, get a good night's sleep, and eat a healthy meal before your appointment to ensure you're in optimal condition.

What should I do for tattoo aftercare?

Aftercare instructions may vary slightly depending on the tattoo artist, but generally include keeping the tattoo clean, applying a recommended ointment or moisturizer, avoiding direct sunlight, and refraining from picking or scratching the tattooed area.
