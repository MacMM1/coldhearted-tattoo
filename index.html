Who We Are

An independent studio, built on trust.

Cold Hearted Tattoo is based in the heart of Brockley Rise, South East London. We're a team of artists with different styles and the same passion — making sure every client feels comfortable from first consultation to final line.

Our Craft

Realism. Fine line. Nothing generic.

We specialise in a wide range of tattoo styles, with a strong focus on realism and fine line work. Bring a clear idea, or want something built from scratch — we work with you until it's exactly right.

The Studio

Founded by Ryan and Alanna.

Cold Hearted was built on a simple idea: great tattooing and genuine human connection go hand in hand. Every artist on the team is handpicked — for the work, and for how they make people feel.
